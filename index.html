ACTIVISION CENTRAL TECH · SOLO C++ PROJECT

Every GCP region on Earth.
Tested down to hop-by-hop MTU.

Starting November 30, 2020, my first project was written 100% solo in C++: deploying virtual machines in every Google Cloud region worldwide to measure real-world latency, jitter, packet loss, traceroute, path MTU, MTU at every hop, packet bursting, and bandwidth across wired, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.

It began on Windows paired with an external NMEA GPS puck and was rapidly ported to Android with native GPS. Driving on vacation from Los Angeles to San Francisco, I ran two laptops side by side — personal Verizon vs. corporate AT&T hotspot — proving Verizon switched cleanly to its Bay Area gateway halfway north while AT&T stayed pinned to Los Angeles.
